Scope
-----

- Live XAU/USD spot via keyless Gold API, cached locally.

- Price-alert dialog with local persistence.
- WorkManager checks enabled price alerts every 15 minutes with network constraints and posts a notification when a threshold is crossed.
- Alerts re-arm after the price leaves the threshold condition.

Accuracy / disclosure
---------------------
- The free exchange-rate source supplies a reference mid-rate, not a named Egyptian bank bid/ask quote.
- The two visual buy/sell cells therefore show the same honest reference rate rather than fabricating a spread.
- Gold values are indicative international spot-derived values; local retail price and workmanship can differ.
- The approved reference page already contains an explanatory disclaimer and the operational info dialog makes the source semantics explicit.
